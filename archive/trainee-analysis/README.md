# Trainee analysis archive

These tables document the sequence-split trainee analysis. In 100 replays of its regression splitter, records from every one of the 34 patients appeared in both training and testing. The reported AUROC of 0.754 was the mean of the first 100 logged test epochs, spanning five folds. It cannot measure discrimination in new patients.

The archived attention weights match a seeded random draw for all 239,634 rows. The rankings derived from them have no biological interpretation. Historical figures 4 and 5 also include simulated curves. The current study uses measured held-out patient predictions and observed training losses.
